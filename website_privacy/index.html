FIELDPATH Website Privacy Policy

These policies apply only to the fieldpathapp.com website (including subpages). They do not apply to the FieldPath mobile app, which has its own privacy policy.

1. Controller

The data controllers are Šimon Drápal and Petr Drahoš, Boženy Němcové 262, Chrast u Chrudimi 53851, Czech Republic.

Contact: legal@fieldpathapp.com

2. What data we process

  • Technical data needed to run the website (for example IP address in server logs, browser type).
  • Analytics data about website use (Firebase Analytics), if you consent — for example page views and clicks.
  • Data related to YouTube embeds, if you consent to loading them.
  • Email content and contact details you voluntarily send to support@fieldpathapp.com or business@fieldpathapp.com.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Operating and securing the website — legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Analytics and YouTube embeds — consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw consent anytime in cookie settings.
  • Replying to messages — contract performance / pre-contract steps or legitimate interest, depending on context.

4. Cookies and trackers

Essential preference storage (language, cookie choice) is used to run the website. Firebase Analytics and YouTube run only after you choose Accept.

You can change cookie settings via the Cookie settings link in the website footer.

5. Recipients and transfers

Depending on selected services, data may be processed by providers: Google (Firebase Analytics, YouTube), website hosting. These providers may process data outside the EEA; where they do, appropriate safeguards are used (for example Standard Contractual Clauses).

6. Retention

  • Server logs: for as long as needed for operation and security, typically weeks to months.
  • Analytics: according to Firebase / Google Analytics settings and the validity of your consent.
  • Email communication: for as long as needed to handle the request and any related follow-up.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent. You may lodge a complaint with the Czech Data Protection Authority (uoou.cz).

Exercise your rights at legal@fieldpathapp.com.

8. FieldPath app

Privacy for the mobile app is described separately: App Privacy Policy.

9. Changes

We may update these policies. The current version is always on this page.
