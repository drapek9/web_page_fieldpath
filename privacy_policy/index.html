Privacy Policy of the FIELDPATH App

These privacy policies apply to the FIELDPATH mobile application (iOS and Android) only. They do not apply to the marketing website fieldpathapp.com. Website privacy is described separately in the Website Privacy Policy.

Tyto zásady platí pouze pro mobilní aplikaci FIELDPATH (iOS a Android). Neplatí pro marketingový web fieldpathapp.com. Ochrana údajů webu je popsána samostatně v Zásadách ochrany osobních údajů webu.

Categories of Personal Data we process in the app

  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • Username
  • Profile picture
  • Age / date of birth
  • Gender
  • Country / state
  • Nationality
  • Matches (match statistics such as goals, assists, minutes played)
  • Training sessions and individual sessions
  • Player statistics and performance ratings
  • Performance data (football metrics you enter and/or that are derived from your activity)
  • Team / club information
  • Position (optional)
  • Height and weight (optional)
  • Injury / recovery / medical condition notes you enter
  • Photos attached to activities (Premium)
  • Friends / social connections and feed activity
  • Strava-connected activity data you choose to import
  • Purchase / subscription status (Apple, Google, RevenueCat)
  • Push notification tokens and related delivery data
  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data (Google AdMob; Facebook App Events where used)
  • Analytics and diagnostics (Firebase Analytics or similar), including app usage events and technical information
  • AI activity feedback inputs/outputs when you use Premium AI features

Main purposes and processors

  • Account and app features — providing the service you signed up for (profile, matches, trainings, friends, calendar, injuries, Premium features).
  • Payments — Apple App Store, Google Play and RevenueCat process subscription data needed for Premium.
  • Analytics — Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and improve it.
  • Advertising — Google AdMob (and related advertising measurement). On iOS, tracking uses App Tracking Transparency where required.
  • Marketing measurement — Facebook App Events may be used for app event measurement.
  • Strava import — only if you connect Strava; we process the activity data you import.
  • AI feedback — Premium AI summaries are generated from your activity data to provide feedback in the app.
  • Support — messages sent to support@fieldpathapp.com or legal@fieldpathapp.com.
