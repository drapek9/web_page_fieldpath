FIELDPATH

Beyond the Game.

Football is more than just a game. FIELDPATH is for players who want to grow and have fun with football, on the pitch and away from it.

Why FIELDPATH started

FIELDPATH started from the idea of a place for everyone for whom football is more than just a game. For those who want to grow, have fun, and be part of a football community.

We want to connect football players, bring them new possibilities, and create experiences that take their relationship with football further. Because football doesn't end with the final whistle. It connects us off the pitch too, brings us shared experiences, and gives us a reason to come back to it.

We are building FIELDPATH as a brand that connects people around football and gives them more than the game itself.

We want to be part of their journey, on the pitch and away from it.

The FIELDPATH app - the first step

Our first step was the FIELDPATH app. A place where you can record matches and trainings, follow your stats, compare yourself with others, and stay in touch with your mates.

We want to help you follow your own progress, keep track of your football journey, and keep you motivated. That's why we keep developing the app and looking for ways to give you an even better place to record and share your football life.
