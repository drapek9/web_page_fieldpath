Why FieldPath?

Football is more than just matches and training sessions.

It's a journey filled with moments, progress, friendships and memories.

But most of this story gets lost over time. Players forget their progress, their sessions, and the path they have taken.

We built FieldPath because we believe every football player deserves to remember their journey and share it with others.

FieldPath is not just about statistics. It is about your football story.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what players ask most before downloading.

Is FieldPath free?

Yes. Core features - matches, trainings, stats and friends - are free. Premium unlocks advanced tools like AI feedback, activity planning and an ad-free experience.

Is my data safe?

We take privacy seriously. Your data is stored securely and we don't sell it to third parties for advertising. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

Do I have to log after every match?

No, it's up to you. Logging takes just a few minutes, but the more regularly you track, the more useful your form and history become.

Does it work for amateurs and youth players?

Yes. FieldPath is for every football player - from youth and amateur levels to regular league players. Just log the matches and sessions you actually play.

How does Strava integration work?

You connect your Strava account once in the app. Then you pick which activities you want to import into FieldPath and set whether each one is a team training or an individual session.

Can I invite teammates?

Yes. Add friends, compare stats and follow each other's progress. Football is more fun when you share it with your team.
